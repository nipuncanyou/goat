# DAY ONE: the series

**Premise:** An 18-year-old English footballer wakes up with a ~$1M (≈£750K) contract signed. The money is real. Everything that comes with it is the story.

**Format:** 8 vertical reels, ~20 s each, same look and voiceover as Episode 1. Each episode is one *Problem*: a real constraint that turns the headline number into something smaller and more dangerous. The HUD (Contract / Banked / Pressure) carries over between episodes, so the audience watches the same life change.

**Ending:** open cliffhanger.

> Fact-check note: all figures are illustrative. Verify before publishing: agent fee norms (~5–10%), UK income tax and NI bands for the current tax year, contract-length rules for under/over-18s, and typical first-contract sizes. A $1M deal at 18 is the top end for an elite prospect, not the norm, and the series should say so at least once.

## Recurring devices
- **Hook line** that ends every episode and sets up the next.
- **Title card** every episode: `PART n · <TIME OR PLACE>`, then a four-word slam headline (the "CLAIM" card).
- **Beige data screen** (`PROBLEM n · <NAME>`) where the problem is shown as a grid, timeline or counter. This is where the real numbers live.
- **HUD** top-left of the story scenes. Pressure only goes up, except once, in Ep 7.
- **Character:** green cap, grey hoodie, boots. He never talks. Captions and VO address "you".
- **Voice:** short declarative lines, "you" address, one fact per beat.

### Ep 1: PROBLEM 1 · THE CUT *(produced: `day-one-voice-music.mp4`)*
Wakes up, contract on the bed, "It starts now." Title: EVERY DOLLAR HAS A CLAIM. Agent and tax take nearly half.
**Hook:** *"That was the easy part. Now the phone rings."*

### Ep 2: PROBLEM 2 · THE CIRCLE
- **Scene:** Same bedroom, phone buzzing. The kid scrolls: old teammate, cousin, "uncle", ex-coach, a stranger from school.
- **Title card:** PART 2 · WEEK ONE. "EVERYONE KNOWS / YOUR NUMBER."
- **Beat:** Each ask pops up with an amount. He says yes to one, and it costs him. A bar shows **asks total vs. what he keeps**. The asks exceed his take-home.
- **VO:** "Nobody asks for a lot. Everybody asks." / "Nine yeses and you're the one who's broke."
- **Turn:** One person doesn't ask for anything: his old youth coach, who just says "Sign nothing without a lawyer."
- **Hook:** *"He's right. But the car's already in the driveway."*

### Ep 3: PROBLEM 3 · THE CAR
- **Scene:** Dealership, gleaming car, the kid grinning for the first time.
- **Title card:** PART 3 · MONTH ONE. "NEW CAR / OLD MONEY."
- **Beat:** Price on screen; he drives off; the number starts falling. Insurance quote for an 18-year-old, then the club's "please don't drive that to training" email.
- **VO:** "It loses value the second it leaves the lot." / "Insurance for you costs more than a teammate's rent."
- **Hook:** *"The expensive part isn't the car. It's the clock."*

### Ep 4: PROBLEM 4 · THE CLOCK
- **Scene:** Quiet. He sits at the edge of the pitch after training, empty stands.
- **Title card:** PART 4 · YEAR ONE. "THE CAREER / IS SHORT."
- **Beat:** A timeline: a few bright years (his earning window), then a long blank line (the rest of his life). Contract expiry date flashes. First money actually moves into savings: Banked $40K.
- **VO:** "Most of your life, you won't be a footballer." / "These years have to pay for the rest."
- **Hook:** *"Then you hear a pop."*

### Ep 5: PROBLEM 5 · THE FEED
*(Order note: Ep 5 and 6 can swap if you want the injury earlier.)*
- **Scene:** A clip of one good match goes viral. Followers rocket. Then one bad match: the comments turn.
- **Title card:** PART 5 · MONTH 14. "EVERY TOUCH / IS CONTENT."
- **Beat:** Follower counter climbs, then a sentiment bar goes from green to red in one game. Pressure to 68%.
- **VO:** "Millions of strangers have an opinion of you." / "None of them know you."
- **Hook:** *"On Saturday, he's still the one they want."*

### Ep 6: PROBLEM 6 · THE KNEE
- **Scene:** Training session, a tackle, he doesn't get up.
- **Title card:** PART 6 · MONTH 18. "ONE TACKLE / EVERYTHING."
- **Beat:** Medical room. The beige screen shows his contract with clauses highlighted: what is guaranteed, what depends on appearances. Pressure to 80%.
- **VO:** "A contract is a promise with conditions." / "Read the conditions."
- **Hook:** *"He comes back. Someone's waiting with a plan."*

### Ep 7: PROBLEM 7 · THE FRIEND
- **Scene:** A charming old friend, new watch, a "can't-lose" investment: restaurant / crypto / property.
- **Title card:** PART 7 · MONTH 22. "GUARANTEED / ISN'T."
- **Beat:** A chart that only goes up, then a cliff. Banked drops from $40K to $15K. Pressure *falls* to 60% (he's relieved to have lost it all at once), the only time it drops.
- **VO:** "If it's guaranteed, ask why they need *your* money." / "He learns it for $25K."
- **Hook:** *"Then the manager posts the team sheet."*

### Ep 8: PROBLEM 8 · THE ENVELOPE (cliffhanger)
- **Scene:** Team sheet pinned up. He scans it. Cut to his phone: a bid from a rival club, **and** a sealed envelope from the club doctor on the same day.
- **Title card:** PART 8 · MONTH 24. "TWO ANSWERS / ONE DAY."
- **Beat:** Two doors: the offer and the envelope. HUD values flicker to `?`. He reaches for one. Cut to black.
- **VO:** "You have what you wanted. Now what?" End card: **TO BE CONTINUED**.
